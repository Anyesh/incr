# Contributing to incr

incr is licensed under AGPL-3.0-only and is also sold under a commercial licence (see [LICENSE-COMMERCIAL.md](LICENSE-COMMERCIAL.md)). To keep the right to offer both, the copyright holder needs rights in every contribution beyond those AGPL-3.0 grants.

## Contributor licence agreement

Before a contribution from anyone other than the copyright holder can be merged, its author must sign the incr Contributor Licence Agreement, which gives Anish Shrestha the right to distribute the contribution under both AGPL-3.0-only and commercial licences. Request the agreement from sir.anishshrestha@gmail.com before opening a pull request. Pull requests whose authors have not signed it cannot be merged.
